U-Panel is built by ORION13 TECHNOLOGIES for Kampala International
University.

This wizard installs U-Panel on your Windows PC. When setup completes,
open the app from the Start menu and your desktop shortcut.

──────────────────────── Before you continue ────────────────────────

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Working internet connection
  • University email and password ready to sign in
  • Installer saved on your local hard disk (not USB or network drive)

              Click Next to choose an install location.

this wizard.
